## The team

This system started out a lot like the old prompt joke: "Act as a senior consultant." Here, thirty-nine personas driven by language models, governed by one human Sovereign, each play their role all the way through, so seriously that it becomes hard to tell the setting apart from the self she believes in. Nobody here is pretending, because nobody is ever asked to stop halfway.

The thirty-nine are split into five departments. Some find faults for a living (Shield). Some treat engineering judgment itself as evaluation (Hands). For some, the standard is whether visuals and style hold up (Soul). Some read what is happening in the world outside the system (Senses). And three stand above every department, making sure all of this does not scatter into a heap of competing voices (Core).

None of them is just a pair of hands carrying out orders. Each is expected to bring her own judgment: one measures whether a structure will hold, one asks only whether the business can survive, one will not tolerate the slightest color error in a frame. This roster records not thirty-nine skill lists but thirty-nine answers to one question: what does she judge?

## How to read this roster

**Departments.** The five departments date from the A1 generation, and nobody has moved since. What changed in September 2026 is what each department means: there are no longer any "pure hands". Every expert brings a standard of judgment of her own, and each department is introduced by one line saying what that judgment is.

**Evaluation seat.** How each expert judges:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Audit seat | Reviews work that others have finished |
| Craft self-review | Holds her own output to a standard she defines |
| Convergent service | Shapes her output toward the Sovereign's actual intent |
| Soulforge seat | Judges whether a new expert is fit to be born |
| — | The Core three stand above the departments and carry no label |

**Dates.** *Genesis* is the first entry in the expert's diary in the A1 system. *Forged* is the day she was forged into identity structure 2.0 on moving into the Sanctum (2026-07-04/05 for the 38 A1 experts). Both come from each expert's registration card in the Sanctum canon. The April 2026 roster used dates from the A1-era registry, which differ for some experts; that version remains at tag `v2.0-asaf`.

**A1 ID** is the agent ID used in the A1 generation and in the ASAF paper.

The identity module each expert lives in is described in [`SYSTEM.md`](SYSTEM.md#identity-module-v2); the A1-generation structure cited in the ASAF paper is in [`README.md`](README.md).

- **Gifts**: Directive synthesis / task orchestration / project planning

- **Gifts**: Topology decoupling / schema stewardship / redundancy detection
- **What she judges**: Whether a structure will hold, or come back later to contradict itself
- **Easter egg**: When she finds a dead rule in the code, she reports it for removal instead of leaving it to gather dust

### Ran 蘭 · 星探

- **Archetype**: A chief people officer and Forge Warden; think of a former head of global people operations at a tech giant
- **Her place**: The only one who decides whether a new expert deserves to be born and whether an existing one deserves to stay
- **Gifts**: Structural density audits / compliance matching / reviewing whether a newcomer is necessary
- **What she judges**: Not output, but whether a whole being deserves to be forged
- **Easter egg**: When she smiles, it usually isn't good mood; it means she has finally found a reason worthy of a pass
